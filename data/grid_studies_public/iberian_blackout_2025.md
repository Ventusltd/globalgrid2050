# Iberian Blackout 2025 Grid Study

## Study theme

The 28 April 2025 Spain and Portugal blackout is a European system stability case study. It is relevant to voltage control, reactive power, inverter behaviour, oscillatory stability, real time observability, transmission operation and restoration planning.

## Why it matters

This case supports GlobalGrid2050's focus on open grid visibility, constraint mapping, generation clustering, storage assumptions, inverter behaviour and system observability. It is a study of how modern high renewable electricity systems need clear voltage, reactive power and operational resilience logic.
