# V9.5.1 — Newspaper volume restoration with canonical signals

V9.5.1 is a separate application. V9.4 and V9.5 remain frozen.

The V9.5 regression was structural: V9.4 displayed 125 V5 discovery headlines and implemented a relevance filter, while V9.5 replaced the newspaper with an eight-item canonical V6 asset and left a visible `RELEVANT` button without its filtering branch.

V9.5.1 separates discovery from assertion:

- `ALL` displays 133 deduplicated headlines: every one of the 125 V9.4 baseline stories plus eight V6 stories.
- `RELEVANT` displays 45 canonical `PRIMARY_MATCH` stories.
- Only canonical stories indexed by exact `repd_ref` may drive a project `NEWS SIGNAL`.
- Discovery-only stories remain visible but cannot confirm a project, status, capacity or date.
- Related-development records remain context only.

The confirmed GOV.UK headline `Beacon Fen Energy Park development consent decision announced` resolves to Low Carbon Limited's 400 MW solar record, REPD `13599`, at 91% matcher confidence. The related 600 MW battery record, REPD `13600`, does not inherit the signal.

The deterministic feed is `dist/major_project_news_v9_5_1.json`, built by `scripts/build_news_feed_v9_5_1.py`. The build fails if the V9.4 125-headline baseline is truncated, the canonical union changes unexpectedly, or Beacon Fen binds to the wrong REPD record.
