# UK Energy Tracker Purpose

Posted: 2026-06-04 23:51 GMT

## Question

Why does the UK energy tracker look like a VENTUS Ltd system, what has VENTUS Ltd built that does not already exist and how can it be used in investor education?

---

## Direct answer

The UK energy tracker looks like a VENTUS Ltd system because it connects engineering reality, market price behaviour, BESS logic, grid stress and commercial decision making in 1 place.

Many platforms show prices. Many platforms show maps. Many platforms show project lists. Many platforms show financial models. The VENTUS Ltd approach is different because it joins the following layers:

* electricity price volatility

* grid choke point reasoning

This makes GlobalGrid2050 a commercial engineering education machine, not only a data dashboard.

## Why this matters for BESS

A BESS is not justified by average electricity price.

A BESS is justified by:

* price spread
* spread frequency
* charge and discharge timing
* round trip efficiency
* degradation cost
* grid charges
* connection cost
* route and transformer constraints
* location near useful choke points
* project execution quality

The investment thesis can be stated simply:

Park the battery near choke points. Charge when electricity prices are negative or low. Discharge when electricity prices are positive enough to cover losses, degradation, charges, connection costs and a target margin.

The tracker makes this visible.

## Investor education use

The tracker can be used in investor meetings to move the discussion from general belief to observable market behaviour.

A suggested sequence is:

1. Show the 10 year GB electricity price view.

Explain that stable price assumptions are no longer enough for investment decisions.

2. Show negative price days.

Explain that solar alone is increasingly exposed to cannibalisation when generation exceeds system demand or local export capacity.

3. Show intraday spreads.

Explain that spread is the BESS revenue signal.

4. Show a 1 week live period.

Explain the real behaviour: charge during low or negative periods and discharge into high value periods.

5. Connect the price view to GIS and grid location.

Explain that not all BESS locations are equal. Location near constraints, substations, demand centres, weak points or future load growth can create strategic value.

6. Connect the location to engineering.

Explain that the commercial case still depends on cable routing, transformer capacity, protection, losses, grid compliance, energisation risk and operational reliability.

---

## Practical decision rule

A BESS project should not be assessed only by headline capacity, duration or battery cost.

It should be assessed by:

* where it sits in the grid
* what price spreads it can access
* how often those spreads occur
* whether the grid connection allows the commercial behaviour
* whether the electrical design can deliver the intended operation safely
* whether the margin remains after losses, degradation and charges

GlobalGrid2050 is being built to make those questions visible before money is committed.

## VENTUS Ltd position

VENTUS Ltd is not using GlobalGrid2050 to present battery storage as a generic opportunity.

VENTUS Ltd is using GlobalGrid2050 to show that BESS value is locational, temporal and electrical.

The battery is valuable when it is placed where the grid needs flexibility, operated when the price spread justifies cycling and engineered so the connection, cable system, transformer and control logic can survive real operation.

This is the reason the tracker looks like a VENTUS Ltd system.

It turns energy market volatility into engineering, procurement and investment logic.
