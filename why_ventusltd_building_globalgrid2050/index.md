## Position

VENTUS Ltd is building GlobalGrid2050 to help the market see the connection between electricity price volatility, grid constraints, BESS value, project location, cable systems, CAPEX, cashflow and long term project risk.

The purpose is not prediction theatre. The purpose is better decision making before procurement, construction and financing assumptions are locked in.
