2 · THE CROSSING SCHEDULE

You do not open-cut a live motorway or a running railway. Those are trenchless crossings, with launch and reception pits set back beyond the asset boundary — so the bore is always longer than the obstacle is wide, and for anything narrow the setback is the dominant term. A 30 m motorway with 15 m setbacks is a 60 m span, not a 30 m drill.

CrossingMethodWhy

Widths, setbacks and cover are the asset owner's requirements and differ between owners — Network Rail and a highways authority do not publish the same numbers — so they are inputs here and never constants. A crossing declared without a width is counted and named, not silently costed at zero.
