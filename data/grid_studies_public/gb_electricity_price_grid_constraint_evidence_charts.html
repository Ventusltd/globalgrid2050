What Changed

Removed

The previous hard coded annual mini charts have been removed from the main evidence page because they could be confused with the raw V6 half hourly chart.

Retained

The page still links to the written study and keeps the official constraint, B6 and gas price setting arguments in the narrative rather than mixing them with raw Elexon price graphics.

Next check

The proper next step is a script that recalculates annual averages, negative half hour day share and annual spread directly from the V6 CSV source and writes a verified JSON output.

Source Discipline

  1. V6 chart engine: live graphics, high and low markers, seasonal colours, daily and half hourly modes.
  2. Annual Elexon CSV path: /data/electricity/elexon_system_prices_YYYY.csv through the V6 loader.
  3. Daily decade aggregate: /uk_energy_tracking_v6/electricity_price_history_daily_decade.json for 12 month, 5 year and 10 year views.
  4. Study overlays: NESO constraint costs, B6 transfer capability and Zakeri and Staffell gas price setting remain separate evidence categories.
