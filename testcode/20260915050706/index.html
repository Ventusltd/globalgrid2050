What was measured

Chrome 153.0.8010.37 used Intel Graphics through ANGLE/D3D11. The desktop viewport was 1440 × 900 at DPR1; touch emulation was 430 × 900 at DPR2. Four-second idle and interaction samples ranged from 166.5 to 171.3 frames per second. Frame interval p95 was 6.1 ms in each case.

No physical phone was tested. These short desktop measurements do not establish sustained thermal performance or operation on a 1 GB device. The harness's NVIDIA utilisation sampler watched a different device from the actual Intel renderer.
