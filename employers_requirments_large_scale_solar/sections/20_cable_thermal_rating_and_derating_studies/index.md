# 20. Cable Thermal Rating and Derating Studies

Cable thermal performance should be verified using recognised calculation methods including those described in IEC 60287.

Cable thermal rating studies should include low voltage AC inverter feeders where multiple circuits are grouped in trenches, ducts, containment systems or switchboard compartments.

The studies should consider installation method, grouping, ambient temperature, soil thermal resistivity, harmonic derating and short circuit withstand.
