Chart Pack

1. Annual price trend

Shows the pre crisis baseline, the 2021 to 2022 gas shock and the higher 2024 to 2026 plateau.

2. Negative price day share

Measures days with at least 1 negative half hourly settlement period, not daily average negative prices.

3. Intraday spread

High minus low daily price spread by year. This is the battery arbitrage signal.

4. Constraint cost pathway

Historical constraint cost outturns and 2030 scenario pathway from official and analyst overlays.

5. B6 transfer gap

Shows the structural gap between present B6 capability and required north to south transfer.

6. Gas price setting share

Zakeri and Staffell comparison: Great Britain versus Germany and France.
