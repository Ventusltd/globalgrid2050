## One-feature release

1. First click on `REPD UPDATED` sorts dated records newest first and displays `▼`.
2. Second click sorts dated records oldest first and displays `▲`.
3. Further clicks toggle between those two directions.
4. `aria-sort`, the retained sort selector and the shareable `sort` URL parameter remain synchronised.
5. Missing official dates remain `not supplied by REPD` and stay after dated records in either direction.
