1 · THE LOAD AS THE PLANT SEES IT

A load does not present kilowatts to a transformer, it presents kilovolt-amperes. Reactive power does no work, but it is carried by the same conductors and occupies the same rating as the real power beside it. That is why a site can be well inside its kW and outside its agreed capacity.

2 · THE CORRECTION, AND WHAT IT GIVES BACK

Correction supplies the reactive power locally instead of drawing it across the network. The site consumes exactly the same energy afterwards and occupies less of its connection. Do not aim at unity: the last few percent costs disproportionately, and a fixed bank sized for full load will over-correct at part load — at which point the site can be charged for exporting reactive power instead.
