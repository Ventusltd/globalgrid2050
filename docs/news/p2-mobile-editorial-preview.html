Historical archive

Older original reporting is available for context but never promoted as newly occurring because of a fresh crawl or changed article headline. No archived articles are loaded in this preview.

Planning register search — not a decision

A planning register search or application listing does not establish that permission was granted, refused or varied. Only an independently checked, dated official decision may appear in the verified decision tier. No search results are loaded in this preview.

Unverified project and article review queue — not headlines

Potential matches, future-dated items, summary-only claims, unsupported second-project associations, unknown event dates and unlicensed articles remain withheld. This preview shows no queue records and makes no claim that a reviewed URL establishes a project event.

Evidence and release requirements

Every public card requires an independently supported REPD-to-article binding, original response hash, independently checked publisher rights, dated evidence and a clear official-versus-attributed tier. This solar-only successor excludes uncleared data-centre payloads. Search, export CSV and clickable MAP-to-engine journeys must pass on the actual staged build at 390px and 1440px, followed by served-byte SHA and rollback. No public-release acceptance is claimed here.

Unknown event dates are displayed as unknown; a year-only document does not supply a specific day. Two projects named in one article require two separately evidenced identity bindings. An article's URL alone does not prove an electrical grid connection.
