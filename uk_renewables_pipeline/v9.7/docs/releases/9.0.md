# V9.0 — Interim export and REPD-date release

V9.0 is a wholesale V8.1 copy with three bounded additions: mobile-accessible filtered CSV export, visible official REPD record-update dates, and Atlas V8 links carrying canonical identity and coordinates.

Atlas V8 does not currently read URL parameters. V9 therefore says “Open Atlas V8” and does not claim automatic record focus. Exact selection requires a future Atlas version with a tested deep-link contract.

V1–V8 are protected by `contracts/legacy-integrity.v9.json`. Those Git hashes are the durable record of prior engineering work; they do not depend on conversational or model memory.
