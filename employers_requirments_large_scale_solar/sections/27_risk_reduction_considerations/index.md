# 27. Risk Reduction Considerations

Risk reduction measures may include:

- improved cable routing to minimise electromagnetic loops  
- coordinated surge protection design  
- insulation monitoring for early detection of leakage currents  
- mandatory low voltage short circuit studies  
- MCCB and protective device rating verification  
- switchboard withstand and type test evidence  
- protection coordination study with final settings  
- cable thermal verification for grouped low voltage inverter feeders  
- explicit earthing and neutral definition  
- commissioning evidence aligned with the final single line diagram
