# V7 product scope

The main market view tracks UK solar above 49 MWp and BESS above 99 MW. Wind is excluded. The default pipeline proposes Application Submitted, Awaiting Construction, Under Construction and Operational records; historical and disputed records remain searchable but cannot silently drive current signals.

The Q2 2026 acceptance fixture contains 384 solar records and 382 BESS records: 766 official records across 718 developments. Record and development counts remain distinct.

Sub-49 MW solar appears only in a separate C&I lane when physical-site evidence supports a commercial or industrial classification. EV charging has its own source and identity contract.
