# Solar periods and retro headings ? 202609052200
Original presentation cartridge. Imports the pinned 202609052155 solar model and its NASA source manifest; no third-party graphics copied. One constant mean irradiance (1361 W/m^2) times Earth's sun-facing disk gives incoming power. Integrating elapsed UTC day, hour and minute gives MWh, not irradiance. Values reset independently at UTC boundaries. These are modelled top-of-atmosphere totals, not live measurements or usable PV output. The grey bar sweeps once a minute at 20 Hz (1 Hz with reduced motion); pause freezes all three totals. Headings receive restrained terminal colours without changing inherited fonts.
