# News and material events

V7 separates a source article, a material event and a project-event assertion. Several articles may corroborate one event; they must not occupy several headline slots as duplicate milestones.

Required event families include consent, CfD, financial close, notice to proceed, construction commencement, grid agreement or delay, energisation, commissioning and commercial operation.

A current article cannot bind to refused, abandoned, withdrawn or expired history through a shared name alone. Planning reference, explicit reapplication, current operator and location evidence must prove continuity. Capacity and publisher quality can corroborate identity but cannot create it.
