1 · STRAIGHT LINE TO CORRIDOR

A straight line is not a route. Buried cable circuits follow the highway network, and across 95 published GB transmission cable circuits that detour measures a factor of 1.245. This is the estate's first pass and it stays exactly as it is — everything else built tonight is additive to it.

2 · WHERE IT REFUSES, AND WHY THAT MATTERS MORE

Below about a kilometre the factor is not measuring route detour at all — it is measuring the distance between two site centroids, and the median error rises to 52.5%. So it returns null, not zero and not a small number. A tool that answers everything cannot be trusted on anything.
