1 · THE RECORD

A fault level is not one number. Make against break, three-phase against single-phase, and a DC offset set by X/R — NESO's ETYS Appendix D publishes eight separately named currents for one busbar, and collapsing them into a generic "fault level" is the error this contract exists to prevent. 2 · QUOTING ONE NAMED METRIC

Once a record is accepted, a figure may be quoted — but only one named metric at a time, with its unit, site, busbar, voltage, publisher, basis and date attached. Nothing here will ever print the bare words "fault level".

The eight ETYS metric names are listed above exactly as data-grid-gb normalises them.
