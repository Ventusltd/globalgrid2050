# V9.6.2 — UK and international newspaper views

V9.6.2 freezes V9.6.1 and adds regional news presentation only. `RELEVANT` is
renamed `UK` while preserving the exact canonical REPD match gate. New
INTERNATIONAL, US and EUROPE views require explicit solar or battery language,
explicit non-UK geography and a UK veto. Regional items are discovery-only and
cannot update a project NEWS SIGNAL.

The release keeps 7,680 UK REPD projects, the full 133-headline newspaper, 45
canonical UK stories and the V9.6.1 mobile horizontal-table repair. Its initial
regional classification contains 19 international headlines: 4 US, 9 Europe
and 6 elsewhere.
