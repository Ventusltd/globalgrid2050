# Iteration 0033

# Kuiper: the light of the one pulse

## What changed in this release

- What stays lit when an application is isolated now takes the colours of the pulse that sweeps the
  disc: white to ice blue, at full brightness. The saturated green is gone. There is one look on this
  page, not two.
- The small record an application's work gathers into is larger and brighter: it fills the inner
  disc, so every dot is bigger while still never wider than a third of the gap to its neighbour.
- The month labels are put away while work is gathered, and come back when it goes home.
- An application spells its name only when it has enough pieces of work to fill every square of
  every letter. With fewer it keeps the small record, which is always readable, rather than a name
  with strokes missing.

One part of the page is released here, and the page's permanent address does not change.
