# Heathrow North Hyde Substation Fire and Critical Infrastructure Resilience Studies

## Study theme

The March 2025 North Hyde substation fire is a UK critical infrastructure resilience case study. It links transmission asset condition, transformer bushings, oil sampling, fire suppression availability, network reconfiguration, private airport electrical distribution, emergency response, public transport disruption and cross sector resilience.

## Public safe note

This page records public sources for technical study. It does not make findings beyond the source documents. Legal, enforcement and liability questions sit with the relevant regulators, authorities and parties.

## NESO North Hyde Review Interim Report

The interim NESO report records that on 20 March 2025 SGT3 at North Hyde 275 kV substation tripped and caught fire, that SGT1 later tripped, that the consequence was loss of all supplies from North Hyde, and that 66,919 customers lost power including parts of Heathrow Airport's private network. The interim report also records that Heathrow used its other electrical connections and reconfigured its internal network before reopening.

https://www.neso.energy/document/360076/download

## NESO North Hyde Review Final Report

The final NESO report is the key technical audit source. Public reporting of the final report records that the fire was most likely caused by moisture entering a high voltage transformer bushing, with an elevated moisture reading detected in July 2018 and mitigating action not implemented. Public reporting also records that a fire suppression system at North Hyde had been identified as inoperable before the incident.
